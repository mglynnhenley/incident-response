B1  File R06_elexon_boalf_acceptances_20190809_1540-1645Z.csv [reported] — bid-offer acceptance
    level records (BOALF) with acceptance times 15:40 to 16:45 UTC inclusive.
    Source: Elexon endpoint /balancing/acceptances/all, one query per settlement period 32 to 38
    of 2019-08-09 (each query returns the records valid for that period); retrieved 2026-10-20.
    Licence: Elexon BMRS open data licence. Contains BMRS data (c) Elexon Limited copyright and
    database right 2026.
    Selection: the 1016 records returned include repeats of records spanning several periods; 843
    are unique, and the 363 whose acceptanceTime is 2019-08-09T15:40:00Z to 16:45:00Z are kept.
    They belong to 139 acceptances of 49 BM units. An acceptance in that window whose accepted
    levels lie wholly outside settlement periods 32-38 would not be in these queries.
    Columns: acceptanceNumber, acceptanceTime, bmUnit, nationalGridBmUnit, settlementDate,
    settlementPeriodFrom, settlementPeriodTo, timeFrom, levelFrom, timeTo, levelTo, soFlag,
    storFlag, deemedBoFlag, rrFlag. Each record is one segment of an accepted level profile:
    levelFrom (MW) at timeFrom to levelTo (MW) at timeTo. acceptanceTime, timeFrom and timeTo are
    UTC at minute resolution. soFlag, storFlag, deemedBoFlag and rrFlag are the publisher's flags
    (system operator, STOR, deemed bid-offer and replacement reserve flags). An acceptance is an
    instruction, not a measurement of what the unit delivered.
    Each acceptance is also an entry in the control log of room ESO-ENCC (query_control_log);
    entry IDs have the form R06-BOALF-<bmUnit>-<acceptanceNumber>.
