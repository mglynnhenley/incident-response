A3  Reading the reports (standard METAR code): DDHHMMZ is day, hour and minute UTC; AUTO marks an
    automated report; dddffKT or dddffGggKT is wind direction (degrees), mean speed and gust
    (knots), and dddVddd a varying direction; a 4-figure group is visibility in metres (9999 = 10
    km or more); TS thunderstorm, RA rain, SH showers, a leading - light; RE (as in RETSRA) recent
    weather, observed since the previous report but not at report time; FEW, SCT and BKN are cloud
    amounts with base in hundreds of feet; CB cumulonimbus, TCU towering cumulus; / marks an
    element the automated system did not observe; nn/nn is temperature and dew point (deg C);
    Qnnnn is pressure (hPa).
