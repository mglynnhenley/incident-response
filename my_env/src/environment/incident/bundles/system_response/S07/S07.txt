R1   1,240 MW [reported] of actions taken by the ESO, using Short Term Operating Reserve, Rapid Start,
     Balancing Mechanism actions and Fast Reserve (from BM and non-BM providers).
R2   Automatic services: static Low Frequency initiated Firm Frequency Responses designed to be triggered
     at 49.7 Hz [reported]; EWIC and Moyle interconnectors initiated at 49.6 Hz [reported]; a service
     contracted with Gas Turbines initiated at 49.6 Hz [reported] and 49.5 Hz [reported] respectively,
     depending on the agreement between NGESO and the providers.
R3   Manual actions by the NGESO control room: rapid start service instructed to one Dinorwig unit;
     instructions to another Dinorwig unit and two Ffestiniog units from a synchronising position to
     generating; an additional distribution connected diesel generator; 400 MW [reported] of short term
     operational reserve instructed.
R4   To cover the enduring loss of generation at Little Barford and Hornsea One, Coryton South and
     Carrington were both instructed by NGESO.

PART 5 — POST-EVENT ANNOTATIONS (attributed; not log content)
Annotation A — ESO technical report dated 6 September 2019, section 6.1:
A1   ESO control room commenced communication with DNOs and NGET control room within
     2 minutes [reported] of the initial fault.
A2   Post-event analysis indicated the earliest restoration was completed at 17:07 [reported] and the
     last customers were reconnected at 17:37 [reported].
A3   Operational communications were generally efficient and effective during the demand restoration
     process and in the demand loss and restoration reporting process; the ESO control room experienced
     no communication issues with customers or with communication systems.
A4   Some DNOs incorrectly called the NGET control room; NGET control room correctly referred DNOs to the
     ESO, with no impact on the restoration process.
A5   Indications are that DNOs did ask specific permission from the ESO control room to restore demand.
     While verbal instructions to DNOs to restore demand did not follow agreed phraseology, they were
     clear and understood.
