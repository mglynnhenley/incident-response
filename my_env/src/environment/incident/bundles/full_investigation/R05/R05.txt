CSV COLUMNS (UTF-8, comma-separated, LF line endings, one header row, long format)
time_as_printed: the time on the chart's own time scale, in the chart's own format (see SERIES).
time_bst_iso: time_as_printed as ISO-8601 with +01:00 appended; empty where the axis does not print
  an absolute time for each sample (APP Figure 3).
value, unit: the digitised value [digitised] and its unit (kV, MW or MVAr).
uncertainty: half-width of the plausible range of value at that time, same unit (see UNCERTAINTY).
series_id: <chart id>/<legend name as printed, spaces replaced by underscores>.
sample_index: category index (APP charts: 0 = first plotted sample) or resampling index (ESO charts).
time_uncertainty_s: uncertainty of the printed time scale itself, in seconds (see CLOCK BASIS).
flags: empty, or ';'-separated: ambiguous_overlap (stroke overlaps others of similar colour; the
  uncertainty includes this), at_axis_top / at_axis_bottom (stroke touches the plot edge, so the
  plotted value may be clipped).

METHOD
1. Each chart's embedded raster image was extracted losslessly from the PDF with PyMuPDF at its native
resolution (APP Figure 3 about 196 dpi, Figure 4 about 223 dpi, ESO Figure 7 about 150 dpi, Figure 8 about 96
dpi on the page). A MuPDF render of the same page region agrees with the raster apart from resampling at line
edges, so nothing else on the page (clip paths, vector drawings) alters the chart. Rendering at a higher
resolution only interpolates these pixels and adds no information.
2. Axes were calibrated from their printed ticks: tick positions measured in the image (gridlines, or the
centres of the tick labels where the axis has no gridlines), tick values as printed, and a least-squares
straight line fitted per axis (at least five ticks per axis; residuals under CALIBRATION).
3. Curves were extracted by colour. Each series' colour and stroke width were measured from its legend swatch.
Call-out boxes, arrows and dashed lines (drawn on top of the charts) were masked; gridlines were modelled as
background. The chart was then re-drawn from candidate vertex positions with every series in legend order
(later series on top) and the positions chosen that best reproduce the image: dynamic programming over stroke
positions found in the image, then refinement to 0.05 px. For the APP charts the vertices are the plotted
samples; for the ESO charts one vertex per pixel column, resampled every 5 ms (about one stroke width).
4. Checks: the digitised values were drawn back onto the source images and inspected; ESO Figure 7 and Figure
8 voltages (the same quantity) were digitised independently and agree. No digitised value was adjusted to
agree with any value stated in a report's text.

UNCERTAINTY
The uncertainty column combines in quadrature, in pixels converted to the series' unit: the axis fit's rms
residual; half the stroke width (the plotted value lies somewhere within the drawn line); colour ambiguity
(the half-width of the range of positions that reproduce the image about as well as the chosen one: small for
a clearly visible stroke, large where strokes of similar colour overlap or an annotation hides the line); and
the local slope times the time-axis fit residual. It does not include the uncertainty of the time scale itself
(time_uncertainty_s) or any error in the original measurement.
APP p40 Figure 3: kV median 0.2, max 1.3; MW median 5.8, max 9.8; MVAr median 5.8, max 12.
APP p41 Figure 4: kV median 0.17, max 0.43; MW median 1.6, max 3.8.
ESO p19 Figure 7: kV median 0.41, max 1.5; MW median 4.6, max 42.
ESO p19 Figure 8: MVAr median 10, max 32.

CLOCK BASIS
No chart prints a time zone, and no chart states which recorder or clock produced its time scale or how that
clock was synchronised. APP Figure 3 prints only minutes, seconds and tenths ('52:33.0'); the hour comes from
its title ('16:52'). Five consecutive samples share each label, so a sample's time is known only to about 0.1
s from its label; the labels do not say whether they are truncated or rounded. The spacing of about 20 ms per
sample is an inference from five samples per 0.1 s label, not printed. time_bst_iso is left empty for this
chart.
APP Figure 4 prints a time to the millisecond for every sample, with hour 16.
ESO Figures 7 and 8 print tick labels to 0.01 s, with hour 16; times between ticks are read from the fitted
scale. In time_bst_iso the printed hour is read as BST; this is an interpretation, not a statement on the
charts.
