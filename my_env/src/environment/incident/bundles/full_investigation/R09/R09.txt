B2   ESO technical report, section 6.4 'Communications by industry and consumers', 'Media
    statements by others', quoted:
    "In the ~90 minutes immediately following the initial fault on the network (starting at [reported]
      17.09hrs) statements were issued by some DNOs and rail companies stating that the loss of
      power and subsequent disruption was the result of a National Grid fault." [reported]
    "These statements were issued ahead of any communication by the ESO and without engagement or
      coordination with the ESO communications team." [reported]
    "These public-facing communications did not impact the speed with which the incident was
      resolved but did drive a large volume of media queries to the ESO." [reported]
B3   ESO appendices, Appendix M, question 35 and the ESO's answer, quoted:
    "What issues did you encounter with the public-facing communications by industry, notably in
      terms of the content and timelines for the messaging?" [reported]
    "The initial communications from some DNOs and rail companies said that the incident had been
      caused by a National Grid fault." [reported]
    "These communications were issued very shortly after the event (from 5.09pm onwards)." [reported]
    "The ESO prepared an external communication seeking to give reassurance to the public which
      was issued at 18:27 once it was established that the DNOs had reconnected all customers." [reported]
    "The initial statements from some parts of the industry were issued before our statement and
      without any co-ordination with our comms team." [reported]
B4   Energy Emergencies Executive Committee, GB power system disruption - 9 August 2019, interim
    report (cover dated September 2019), quoted:
    "DNOs led local public communications during the event and the ESO did not publish public
      statements until after the disruption, meaning that energy partners (such as generators)
      were not directly informed." [reported]
    "In parallel with social media, DNOs reached out to customers via text message alert services
      and telephone services between 17:02 and 18:30." [reported]
    "Updates from the ESO followed the first set of restorations and were not publicly released
      until 18:27 via Twitter." [reported]
    "The Secretary of State for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy later announced via
      Twitter (10th August) and GOV.UK (14th August) that the government was to commission the
      Energy Emergencies Executive Committee to provide an interim and extended report into the
      power disruptions." [reported]
B5   Energy Emergencies Executive Committee, GB power system disruption on 9 August 2019: Final
    report (January 2020), section 5 'Communication channels', quoted:
    "The first public announcement from ESO was issued at 18:27, which was following the event and [reported]
      once the system had stabilised." [reported]
    "By this time, many DNOs had used twitter to provide area updates that were widely
      circulated." [reported]
    "However, electricity generators received no communication during the events of 9 August that
      would have made them aware of the system condition." [reported]
B6   Ofgem, 9 August 2019 power outage report (3 January 2020), paragraph 3.23, quoted:
    "We have also found issues with Hornsea 1’s communication with the ESO. Hornsea 1 did not

      process of starting up two of its modules (temporarily) without any coordination with the
      ESO whilst the ESO was attempting to respond to the generation losses it was aware of." [reported]
