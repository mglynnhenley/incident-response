PART 4 — POST-EVENT ANNOTATIONS (attributed; added after the event, not SOE content)
Annotation A — operator report "RWE Preliminary Findings Regarding the Event on 9th August 2019",
dated 30 August 2019:
A1  Steam turbine: "Our investigations have confirmed that the Steam Turbine tripped due to a
    discrepancy in the speed signals after the point of the Transmission System fault clearance."
A2  Steam turbine: a comprehensive investigation of hardware, software, fault handling and diagnostic
    coverage for the conditions the ST was subjected to during this rare system disturbance is ongoing;
    additional high-resolution frequency data relevant to Eaton Socon has been received from NGESO.
A3  Gas turbines: upon initiation of the steam turbine trip the gas turbines went into bypass mode of
    operation, which is the normal response to allow sustained operation.
A4  GT1A: "For reasons presently unknown a high-pressure excursion occurred on GT1A resulting in an
    automated trip." The operator adds that given the prevailing conditions all systems functioned as
    expected.
A5  GT1B: "GT1B was manually tripped shortly after as a result of excessive steam pressure."
A6  Gas turbines: "A physical inspection of the bypass system is now planned during the forthcoming
    outage in September 2019 to help determine the root cause of the pressure excursions."
A7  UPS: all UPS units at site functioned correctly to enable continuity of supply to plant equipment;
    the relevant OEMs confirmed this; the operator does not now believe the UPS were related to the
    subsequent trip.
A8  UPS: "RWE's hypothesis is that the UPS' have initiated a changeover to battery back-up supplies as a
    consequence of the system disturbance." The exact characteristic of the power supply to the UPS
    causing it to switch to battery operation is still to be determined and subject to further investigation.
A9  UPS: OEM resilience testing requested during the outage commencing 6 September 2019 [reported],
    cross-referenced to manufacturing tests (9 November 2017) and commissioning tests (January 2018).
